## The diff against the baseline

`diff B D` gives 22 hunks (`diff B D | grep -E '^[0-9]' | wc -l`); they make seven changes, the rest are their plumbing or the design tool's:

1. **A full replay viewer**: markup `D:245-420` (head `D:248-274`, world HUD `D:284-320`, loading, failed and locked
   states `D:322-340`, roster rail `D:343-365`, transport `D:369-417`) and logic `D:772-1058` (open and close
   `D:824-834`, keys `D:892-941`, state values `D:1017-1019`, `D:1555`), wired by `D:521-525` (the rail keeps the
   focused player in view), `D:573`, `D:637`, `D:1232` (Enter on a Replays row opens it) and `D:1330` (the viewer takes
   the keys while open). The icon list of the Material Symbols link gains the viewer's icons (`D:18`) and the design's
   knobs gain `replayDemo` (Normal / Failed / Locked, `D:440`, read at `D:878-879`), a design-tool switch to show the
   states; neither is built (LR.R9: icons in the launcher's own set, no CDN, no design knobs).
2. **The Replays panel's rows**: "Your matches · newest first", then "Map · Mode" / "Won|Lost x-y · date · partial" /
   length, each opening the viewer (`D:1195-1196`, in place of `B:726-727`).
3. **The pad map**: START -> Space, L1 -> `j`, R1 -> `l` (`D:735`, in place of `B:552`), the viewer's play/pause and
   10-second steps.
4. **A third background picture**, "SOCOM Legacy" key art, drawn cover with the theme gradient (`D:7`, `D:664-668`,
   `D:1204`).
5. **The logo PNG** in place of the emblem and the SOCOM II / LEGACY words (`D:28`, in place of `B:24-30`), and the SL
   favicon and touch icon (`D:8-9`). The design tool's thumbnail changes to match (`D:13`).
6. **A dim scrim over the shell while the viewer is open** (`D:24`, .45s), and the crossbar, the panel and the status
   marks to opacity 0 (`D:1541`, `D:1547`, `D:1560`).
7. **`white-space:nowrap` on the hint keys** (`D:189`).

Everything else is the baseline.

**Code in the design that never runs is not built**: the centred "check" modal with its DEPLOY pill (`D:207-243`,
`D:1561-1598`; nothing sets `check: true` -- `openCheck`, `D:1064-1069`, opens the "Before you deploy" panel), the halo
(`D:46`, `display:none`), `initials` and `goProfile` (`D:1557`, `D:1559`).

**What the design does not have**: a share button, match history, a leaderboard or stats screens, a Mods screen, the
disc Share panel. RB-14, RB-20, RB-25..28 and our Mods screen come from the owner's text of 2026-11-08 (RB.R1), not
from the design.
