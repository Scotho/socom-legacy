Sony provides this line art for game and app developers. Its usage instructions permit
extracting parts to explain controls and require accurate depiction. The light front
view was extracted from the key-assignment sheet and converted from PDF-compatible
Illustrator vectors to SVG. The page background and alternate dark view were omitted.
The retained artwork paths are unchanged. Live input indicators are a separate UI layer.
