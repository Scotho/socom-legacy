**Status: Forge, local only.** A custom map is added in Home > Experiments > Mods, opened in the Forge and walked in
the test room, all on your device. It never reaches an online match. This page describes builds on engine contract
0.1.2 or later (the Forge path's first slice); a build on 0.1.1 does not read a bundle.

A **custom map** is a map someone made in the Forge, shared as one file: a **Forge map bundle**. You download one zip,
drop it into Mods, and the map turns up in the Forge and the test room under **Custom Maps**. You never install its
packs one by one: the bundle carries them.

## What is inside a bundle

A bundle is a zip with two things in it:

- **`forge-bundle.json`**, the bundle's label: which map this is (a Forge layout) and which packs it carries.
- **The packs**, each whole in its own folder (`packs/0/`, `packs/1/`, ...). Each is an ordinary v2 pack, with its own
  manifest `s2u-pack.json` ([Pack anatomy](/anatomy/)). A pack that holds a map carries it as a **content map**: the
  map's ground, spawns and props, and the 3D model drawn for it.

Nothing in a bundle is a program: it is data, checked before anything uses it, like every pack
([What a pack is](/what-is-a-pack/)).

## Try the sample yard

The **sample yard** is a small bundle to try: a floor, three cover blocks and two team starts, in one pack
(`example.sample-yard` 0.1.1). It is synthetic and **CC0** (public domain): made from scratch, with no game data and
nothing from any other game. It sits in the project's repository at `socomlegacy/test-fixtures/creator/sample-yard.forge.zip`.

*Download: not published on this site yet.* Its download link is added when the wiki is next published.

## In Mods

Drop the zip into **Home > Experiments > Mods**, as you would any pack ([Testing a pack locally](/guides/test-locally/)).
Mods checks the bundle, installs the packs it carries (a pack you already have is not added twice) and keeps the map.
It says: "Sample Yard added: a custom map for the Forge and the test room."

The map gets its own row:

| what the row shows | for the sample yard |
|---|---|
| the kind | "Forge map bundle" |
| the map, its packs, whether it is ready | "Sample Yard · 1 pack · Ready" |
| where it opens | "Custom map · Forge, test room" |

**Ready** means every pack the map needs is on this device. When one is not, the row says so, by name -- for
example "Needs 1 pack not here: example.props 1.0.0". Add the missing pack in Mods and the row reads Ready.

The row has no on/off switch: a custom map is opened from the Forge or the test room, not switched on. **Remove**
takes the map away and keeps its packs, because other maps may use them: "Sample Yard removed. Its packs are kept:
other layouts may use them." Dropping the same bundle again changes nothing: "Sample Yard is already here: nothing
changed."

## In the Forge

The Forge's base list, and the list **New** shows when it asks what a layout starts from, gain a group named
**Custom Maps**, after the Forge's own groups. It lists:

- the bundles you installed in Mods;
- the maps inside packs you installed on their own (with no bundle);
- your saved layouts.

Each row shows its name and a short note: "Sample Yard · Ready", or "· needs 1 pack". Hold the pointer over a row for
the whole sentence. Choosing a row opens it and says the same sentence once ("Ready", or "Needs 1 pack not here: ...").

An installed bundle opens **read-only**: it belongs to Mods, and nothing you do changes it. The toolbar's **Saves**
button becomes **Save as**: it asks for a name ("This custom map is installed and read-only: a copy is saved in this
browser, and opened to edit."), saves a copy among your own layouts, and opens that copy for you to edit. The Forge's
Play runs the map on your device, as it does any layout.

## In the test room

The test room gets a second list beside its map list, also named **Custom Maps**, with the same rows. Choosing one
loads the map ("reading the custom map") and lets you walk it: its ground, its spawns and its props. There are no
rules, no characters and no objectives: the test room is for walking, and the Forge is for playing.

**Offline only.** Online, the test room joins a server room named after the map, so a custom map's ground would go
with you into a room other players share. While the test room is set to a server, the list is switched off and says
"custom maps walk offline only".

## Never online

A custom map never reaches an online match. An online lobby plays the game's own maps and content, and refuses a
layout for either of two reasons, each with its own words:

| the layout | the refusal |
|---|---|
| stands on an authored map (its base is a content map) | `record.ONLINE_CONTENT_MAP`: "An online lobby plays the disc's own maps: this layout stands on an authored map. Play it locally." |
| needs any creator pack, even on one of the game's own maps | `record.ONLINE_PACKS`: "An online lobby plays the disc's own content: this layout needs creator packs. Play it locally." |

The sample yard gets both: it stands on its own authored map, and it needs its pack. Whether a lobby may ever carry
packs is a question for the project's owner (H273); until he answers, they stay out. Play a custom map in the Forge,
or walk it in the test room.

## For creators: the bundle's fields

The envelope `forge-bundle.json` holds exactly four fields; any other is refused.

| field | what it is |
|---|---|
| `schema` | Always `s2u.creator/forge-map-bundle`. |
| `version` | Always `1`. |
| `layout` | A Forge layout record, `forge-layout` version 3: the map. Its `base` is `content:` plus a library stem when it stands on a content map, with `contentBase` (`mapId` and the pack's `ref`) naming that map; its `requires` lists the packs it needs. |
| `packs` | A list of at most 64 `{ref, root}` entries. `ref` is the pack's `id`, `version` and `hash` (the pack digest, 64 lower-case hex digits); `root` is the pack's folder, ending `/`, with `s2u-pack.json` at its top. |

- `forge-bundle.json` sits at the zip's root, or inside one top folder (a zipped folder). Every other file belongs to
  one pack's folder.
- Each pack is checked whole, as any v2 pack is, and must be exactly the pack its `ref` names.
- A pack the layout needs but the bundle does not carry is not an error: it is listed as missing, and Mods' row says
  "Needs ...".
- A content map is `s2u.creator/content-map` version 1 or 2. Version 2 adds one field, `visual.includesProps`: whether
  the map's 3D model already draws its props.
- The fields in full, for an editor: the JSON Schema `socomlegacy/packages/creator-contracts/schemas/forge-map-bundle.v1.schema.json`.
  The contract's validator (`validateForgeBundle`, `creator-contracts/src/bundle.ts`) is the authority. The contract
  version that brings all of this, 0.1.2, is on the [Changelog](/changelog/) and the
  [Manifest reference](/reference/manifest/).
