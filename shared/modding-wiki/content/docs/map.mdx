**Status: Format only.** This page is the wiki's map. The pages it links each carry their own status: the earlier
formats loaded in Home > Experiments > Mods work today, a rules pack runs in the Forge's local Play, and a v2 pack is
checked and stored but not applied. The rows marked *planned* are pages not written yet.

The wiki has three readers. A **player** wants to put a pack in the game. A **creator** wants to make one and share
it. An **agent** (an AI assistant working for either) wants the exact rules in a form it can read without a browser.
Every page ends with a **For agents** section for that third reader (see [For agents](#for-agents-on-every-page)
below), and the whole wiki is indexed in plain text at `/llms.txt` (the site's root).

### Custom maps: the Forge map bundle

A Forge map bundle is a zip whose root (or one folder) holds `forge-bundle.json`: the schema
`s2u.creator/forge-map-bundle` version 1, a Forge layout, and the v2 packs it needs, each whole under its own folder.
A pack in it can carry a content map at the schema `s2u.creator/content-map` version 2. Builds on engine contract
0.1.2 read it (the Forge path's ruling FP.R2, in `socomlegacy/docs/specs/2026-11-07-forge-path-design.md`). The next
format, 0.2, is a proposal the project's owner is reading (`socomlegacy/docs/specs/2026-11-08-pack-format-0.2.md`, the
Forge path's FP-8).

### For agents, on every page

Every page's last section is headed **For agents** and holds one JSON object:

| key | what it holds |
|---|---|
| `page` | The page's address on this site, such as `/anatomy/`. |
| `audience` | Who the page is for: one or more of `players`, `creators`, `agents`. |
| `summary` | One line: what the page says. |
| `inputs` | What a reader brings to the page (a file, a code, a question). |
| `outputs` | What the reader leaves with. |
| `see_also` | The addresses of the pages to read next. |

The wiki's drift test (`shared/modding-wiki/scripts/drift.test.mjs`) fails when a page has no such block, when its
JSON does not parse or its keys differ, or when a page is missing from this map or from `/llms.txt`.
